# SOP Factory — AI-Powered Multi-Sector SOP & Compliance Generator

**Live Web App:** [https://sopfactory-hq.github.io/](https://sopfactory-hq.github.io/)

**SOP Factory** converts raw process notes, past SOPs, internal company standards, handwritten diagrams, or short process walkthrough videos into **>95% audit-scored, multi-page Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)** in seconds.

## Key Capabilities
- **Dual-Maturity >95/100 Audit Gate:** Enforces Document Control, RACI Role Accountability, FMEA Risk Assessment (Severity, Occurrence, Detection, RPN), measurable acceptance limits, and CAPA escalation rules.
- **Auto-Localized Bilingual Operator Guides:** Pairs Simple English with the facility's local official language (Hindi, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, Arabic, etc.) alongside a verification checklist.
- **Multimodal Process Ingestion:** Accepts plain text, PDFs, whiteboard/equipment photos, and short MP4/WEBM walkthrough videos.
- **1-Click ClickUp Sync & Workstation QR:** Publishes structured Markdown SOPs directly into ClickUp and generates a scannable workstation QR code for floor deployment.

## Supported Standards & Frameworks
`ISO 9001:2015` • `ISO 22000` • `ISI (BIS)` • `FSSAI` • `NABH` • `WHO GDP` • `HACCP` • `OSHA` • `SOC 2` • `ISO 27001` • Custom Internal Standards
