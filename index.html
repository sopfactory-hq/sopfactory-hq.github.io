Frequently Asked Questions

How does SOP Factory ensure audit-ready compliance?

Before displaying any blueprint, our backend evaluates your workflow against sector-specific quality gates (ISO 9001, SOC 2, NABH, FSSAI/ISO 22000, HACCP, KYC/AML, or ISO 21001/NAAC) and automatically embeds mandatory verification checks and role sign-offs.

Can I request a specific layout or table structure?

Yes. Enter your formatting preferences in the Layout Preferences & Remarks field before generating, or use the "1. Request Modification" button on the preview screen to refine sections, safety callouts, or table columns.

Which industries and businesses are supported?

SOP Factory is calibrated for Manufacturing, IT & SaaS, Healthcare & Clinics, Hospitality & Hotels, Food & Beverages Manufacturing, Restaurants & Cloud Kitchens, Financial Institutions, and Education & Academic Institutions.
