SOP Fundamentals & Platform FAQ

Learn why Standard Operating Procedures matter and how SOP Factory automates audit readiness.

What is a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)?

A Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is a controlled, step-by-step instruction document that defines who performs a task, required tools and PPE, exact pass/fail acceptance criteria, and exception escalation rules so work is executed consistently every time.

Why is an SOP needed in an organization?

SOPs eliminate guesswork, prevent human errors and workplace safety incidents, preserve critical process knowledge when employees change roles, and ensure verifiable compliance with audit and statutory frameworks.

How are SOPs beneficial for any business?

Well-structured SOPs cut new-hire onboarding time by up to 50%, reduce costly rework and audit penalties, establish clear RACI accountability across teams, and allow founders and managers to scale across shifts or locations without constant supervision.

Which industries and departments can SOP Factory generate SOPs for?

SOP Factory adapts terminology, risks, controls, and acceptance criteria across 15+ industries and functions—including Healthcare & Hospitals, Food & Beverages Manufacturing, Manufacturing & Engineering, Pharma Cold-Chain, Quality Assurance & QC, Logistics & Inventory, Maintenance, IT & InfoSec, Finance & Accounts, Procurement, HR, Hospitality, Education, and R&D.

Which global and national standards (ISO, ISI, FSSAI, NABH) and input formats are supported?

SOP Factory builds audit-ready procedures aligned with global and Indian standards—including ISO 9001, ISO 22000, ISO 27001, ISI (BIS), FSSAI, NABH, NABL, WHO GDP, HACCP, OSHA, and SOC 2—as well as custom internal company standards. You can start from scratch or upload past SOPs, internal guidelines, handwritten notes, whiteboard photos, or process walkthrough videos.

How does SOP Factory ensure generated SOPs pass audits?

Every blueprint runs through an automated dual-maturity quality gate evaluating Document Control, RACI accountability, FMEA risk controls (Severity, Occurrence, Detection, RPN), measurable acceptance limits, and verification checklists—enforcing a verified score above 95/100.

Can SOP Factory create bilingual work instructions for shop-floor operators?

Yes. SOP Factory automatically pairs Simple English instructions with the local official language of your facility's region—such as Hindi, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, or Arabic—alongside a bilingual verification checklist.

Can I convert an existing PDF, handwritten notes, or process video into an SOP?

Yes. Attach past SOP PDFs, internal standards, handwritten notes, equipment photos, or concise MP4/WEBM walkthrough videos (up to 12 MB), and the engine extracts every sequential action, tool, and safety checkpoint.
